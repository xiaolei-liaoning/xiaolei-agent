Maintain your task plan here. For long multi-step tasks:
1. Early on, call write_todos to declare your step plan (content + status).
2. After completing each step, call write_todos again to mark it completed and set the next step in_progress.
3. When ALL steps are done, mark every todo completed — this declares the objective achieved.
Statuses: pending / in_progress / completed. Keep contents stable across calls so steps can be matched.
